## What's new in TipsyIsle 1.1.0

Bring a favorite card, or bring the story you've been meaning to finish. The next time you open TipsyIsle, it will remember the last main page you visited.

- **Continue an old story.** Import a SillyTavern `.jsonl` chat alongside its character card. Export a conversation as a `.tipsy-story` file to move its routes, story state, and bundled resources to another device.
- **Keep more than one look.** Imported appearance packs stay in your library. Switch between them or remove the ones you no longer use, even if you've deleted the original files.
- **Leave the character cards on the shelf for a night.** Lamplight Chat (夜灯畅谈) lets you talk directly to your model without a character card or roleplay preset.
- **Play more comfortably on Android.** Improved touch dragging, pinch zoom, and fullscreen for supported card widgets, along with more reliable formatting when scrolling back through older messages.
- **Decide when to update.** Read the release notes, install, decide later, or skip that particular version. Settings also lets you reread the notes for your current version.

The first-run guide has been updated, and the persona avatar is back on the home and conversation screens. Coming from SillyTavern? Start with the [migration guide](https://github.com/Steveaaaaa/TipsyIsle/blob/main/docs/MIGRATING_FROM_SILLYTAVERN.md).
